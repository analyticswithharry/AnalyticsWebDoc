Supabase Blog System Mind Map

Root: Supabase Schema
├── auth.users (built-in)
│   └── id (UUID PK)
├── profiles
│   ├── id (UUID PK, FK → auth.users.id)
│   ├── full_name
│   ├── avatar_url
│   ├── bio
│   └── timestamps
│   └── RLS: own only
│   └── Trigger: auto-create on signup
├── posts
│   ├── id (UUID PK)
│   ├── user_id (FK → profiles.id)
│   ├── title
│   ├── slug (unique)
│   ├── content
│   ├── image_url
│   ├── published (bool)
│   └── timestamps
│   └── RLS: public read published, owner manage
├── comments (optional)
│   ├── id
│   ├── post_id (FK → posts)
│   ├── user_id (FK → profiles)
│   ├── content
│   └── RLS: on published posts
├── likes (optional)
│   ├── post_id + user_id unique
│   └── RLS: on published posts
└── dashboard_stats (VIEW)
    ├── user_id
    ├── total_posts
    ├── published_posts
    ├── comments_received
    └── likes_received
